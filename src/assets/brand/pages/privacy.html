Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Amaze One Platform

Our Sovereign Privacy Commitment: Amaze One is built from the ground up to guarantee strict data isolation, private infrastructure hosting, and complete user sovereignty. Your communication data, files, and credentials are never sold or shared with third-party advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

To provide secure and dependable communication services across mobile and desktop environments, Amaze One processes the following categories of information:

  • Account Credentials: Username, email address, display name, and securely hashed authentication credentials used to access your Amaze One instance.
  • Communication Data: Messages, channel memberships, direct discussions, threaded conversations, and files or media you voluntarily transmit through the service.
  • Device & Session Information: Client app version, device identifier, IP address, and login timestamps strictly necessary for maintaining active sessions and preventing unauthorized intrusions.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information processed by Amaze One is utilized exclusively to operate, safeguard, and maintain your collaboration environment:

  • Delivering real-time messaging, audio/video calling, and push notifications to your verified devices.
  • Enforcing security safeguards, including role-based access control and brute-force protection.
  • Troubleshooting service anomalies and monitoring infrastructure uptime.

3. Data Ownership & Storage Sovereignty

All communication content and files generated on Amaze One remain the exclusive intellectual and proprietary property of your organization. Amaze One is deployed within private dedicated environments, ensuring your operational data never traverses public analytics pipelines or commercial data brokers.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your team administrator maintains full operational control over message and media retention schedules. Upon account deactivation or upon administrator request, user data and transmitted records are permanently expunged in accordance with your organization’s data governance protocol.

5. Security & Protection Controls

Amaze One implements enterprise-grade technical safeguards including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, strict password hashing, encrypted local database stores on mobile/desktop, and session token rotation to protect against unauthorized access.

6. Contact Us

For any inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data rights, please contact our dedicated security team at support@amazeconnect.com.
